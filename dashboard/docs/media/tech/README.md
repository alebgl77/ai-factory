# Technology marks

AI Factory uses these third-party marks to identify the corresponding software,
protocols and integrations. The marks belong to their respective owners.
AI Factory is an independent project. The asset inventory indicates no
sponsorship, endorsement or certification.

The [Simple Icons collection](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/9e2437ce2d46aa8054d8a00ac63370a44cf5e71a/LICENSE.md)
is distributed under CC0 1.0. Its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/9e2437ce2d46aa8054d8a00ac63370a44cf5e71a/DISCLAIMER.md)
explains that individual logos may carry separate rights and restrictions.
[CNCF artwork](https://github.com/cncf/artwork/blob/bffd512a8b625f15cf5f8e0427469380b5768e90/LICENSE.md)
is subject to the [Linux Foundation trademark policy](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/trademark-usage).
The Kubernetes icon is the project mark. The Certified Kubernetes program uses
a separate mark. The [Langfuse](https://langfuse.com/brand) and
[Qdrant](https://qdrant.tech/brand-resources/) monochrome marks come from their
respective official brand kits. [Twenty](https://twenty.com/terms) permits
truthful reference to its open-source project. The AI Factory MIT license
covers its own work. Third-party brand rights are separate.

The original shapes and colors are preserved inside a neutral plate. The table
